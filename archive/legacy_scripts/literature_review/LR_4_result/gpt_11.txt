Inclusion criteria:
1. Studies must be phase II or III randomized controlled trials (RCTs) with either multicenter or single-center design.
2. Studies must investigate the effect of bempedoic acid on plasma lipids or high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hsCRP).
3. Studies must test the safety of bempedoic acid in short- and middle-term administration and report all adverse events (AEs) that occurred during treatment.

Exclusion criteria:
1. Studies that are not phase II or III randomized controlled trials (RCTs).
2. Studies that do not investigate the effect of bempedoic acid on plasma lipids or high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hsCRP).
3. Studies that do not test the safety of bempedoic acid in short- and middle-term administration.
4. Studies that do not report all adverse events (AEs) that occurred during the treatment.
5. Studies that lack a control-treated group for comparison with bempedoic acid.
