

Inclusion criteria:
1. Studies must measure G6PD activity using ultraviolet (UV) spectrophotometry at a wavelength of 340 nm.
2. Studies must include individual-level data from participants, including those without known haematological conditions, and may include patients with malaria but must be analyzed separately.
3. Studies must provide sufficient details on G6PD activity measurements (U/g Hb) and must not include samples with extreme G6PD activity (>30 U/g Hb) or missing data on G6PD activity or sex.

Exclusion criteria:
1. Studies that only assessed individuals with known haematological conditions.  
2. Family studies.  
3. Studies with insufficient details regarding G6PD activity measurements or study characteristics.  
4. Studies that did not measure NADPH formation at a wavelength of 340 nm.  
5. Samples missing data on either G6PD activity (U/g Hb) or sex.  
6. Samples for which measured G6PD activity was extreme (>30 U/g Hb).
