Prevalence of erythrocyte glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase (G6PD) deficiency in the population of western Turkey.; BACKGROUND: Newborn G6PD deficiency screening has been recognized as an essential component of public health care in most developed and some Mediterranean countries. However, such screening is yet to be widely embraced in Turkey.
